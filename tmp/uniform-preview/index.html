Profession uniforms by civic tier

A complete redesign study for villagers and outpost illagers. Same blocky body, same role identity, clothes that grow with the settlement: rough founding workwear, established village kit, dressed civic uniforms, then prestige. Unemployed villagers, Nitwits, and children are omitted on purpose.

Food & land Wood & stone Craft & arms Civic Outpost illagers

Tier 1 — Founding

Homespun, patched, muted dirt-path and wood. Survival kit.

Tier 2 — Established

Cleaner dyed cloth, cobble-gray trim, brass. Town that has arrived.

Tier 3 — Dressed civic

Tailored cuts, smooth-stone silver, copper clasps. Formal woodsmen and watch.

Tier 4 — Prestige

Gold embroidery, brick-red lining, ceremonial versions of the same job.
